Digital Wedding & Banquet Cash Gift Ledger (សៀវភៅកត់ចំណងដៃអាពាហ៍ពិពាហ៍ឌីជីថល)
Target Users: Banquet receptionists, wedding hosts, and family coordinators.
Core Problem: Recording cash gift envelopes (ចំណងដៃ) at traditional Khmer weddings is stressful. It involves fast-paced handwriting in paper notebooks, manual tallying at midnight, and frequently misreading guest names or amounts when reciprocating years later.
Key Tablet Features:
Rapid Dual-Entry Form: Left pane displays continuous numeric/currency shortcuts (e.g., $20, $30, $50, $100, 50,000៛, 100,000៛), while the right pane shows the running live guest register.
KHQR Reception Display: Integrated dynamic or static KHQR screen facing the guest for cashless transfers.
Instant Reconciliation & Export: Real-time totals split by USD and KHR with zero math errors. Exports directly to Excel or Telegram with searchable guest names and hometowns.
Tablet Advantage: Large numeric buttons prevent fat-finger mistakes during high-traffic reception rushes.
